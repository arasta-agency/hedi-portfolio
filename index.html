Research & Publications

Peer-Reviewed Publication · 2026

Comparative Evaluation of Text Detection and Extraction Methods for Use in Comics

Hedi Hassan · Marek Woda

21st Int. Conference on Dependability of Computer Systems (DepCoS-RELCOMEX 2026) · Wrocław

A comprehensive benchmark evaluating state-of-the-art text detection and OCR pipelines (CRAFT, DBNet, YOLO variants, EasyOCR) on the complex typography, speech balloons, and dense visual layouts of graphic novels.

Abstract: Text extraction from comics presents unique challenges due to irregular typography, non-rectangular bounding boxes, sound effects integrated into artwork, and varied reading orders. This paper establishes a reproducible benchmarking framework comparing character-level, word-level, and segmentation-based deep architectures to identify optimal configurations for automated semantic parsing.
